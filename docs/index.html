Futures positioning (CFTC COT)

Speculators' net position in futures: contracts betting on a rise minus contracts betting on a fall. Positive means they lean long. "vs 3Y" shows where today sits between the lowest (left) and highest (right) net position of the last three years. Click a row for 10 years of history.
